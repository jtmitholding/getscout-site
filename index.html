How it works

  1. Say what you want

    Write it like a message: “Nike Pegasus 41, size 44, under 800 kr”. Or tap the heart on a product in the feed.

  2. Scout checks every day

    It compares offers from shops and comparison sites in Denmark and the EU, and keeps the best price.

  3. You get an alert

    As soon as the price is at or below your target, you get a notification and an email with the link to the offer.

Made for waiting together

Share a watch with a friend and wait for the same pair of shoes together. See what your friends are watching, and copy a watch in one tap.

How Scout is paid

Scout is free. Some links to shops are affiliate links: when you buy through them, the shop may pay Scout a small commission. It never changes the price you pay.

Privacy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Scout watches prices for you and lets you wait for a product with your friends. Here is what we keep, why, and how to delete it.

What we keep

Your name, email and photo from your Google account. Your watches (product and target price) and the alerts sent to you. Your friends and the watches you joined. If you turn on notifications, your browser's notification subscription. Technical error reports, to fix crashes. Without an account, your watch is kept under an anonymous session.

What it is for

Checking prices for your watches, sending you alerts, and showing your name, photo and non-private watches to your friends. Your friends never see your email. Private watches are hidden from them.

Who processes it

Supabase stores your account and data in Stockholm, in the EU. Anthropic (Claude) reads your messages in the chat to understand which product you want. Resend sends the alert emails. Expo hosts the web app. Google signs you in.

What we never do

We never sell your data and never show you ads based on it. This website uses no cookies and no trackers.

Deleting your data

In your profile, “Delete my account” erases your account, your watches, your alerts and your friendships for good.
